# The Midnight Archive: Blueprint

An interactive blueprint for The Midnight Archive, a VRChat world in design by Doghouse Devs. It's a cloud library at night where every book is a readable English Wikipedia article. Open a book, follow its links, and each linked book flies off its shelf to you while a glowing trail draws your path between the shelves.

Live page: https://midnight-archive-vrc.github.io/midnight-archive-blueprint/ (works on phones).

- **Concept art:** the look of the archive, its towers inside and out, and the spirit dog (made with ChatGPT, for mood only).
- **Archipelago plan:** 11 towers, one per top topic of Wikipedia's Vital Articles (Level 5), on floating isles around the academy. Lantern-lit bridges loop past every isle.
- **Tower section:** books line the outside walls, with a walkway around every level; inside, each tower is a cozy space themed by its topic. Pick a floor to see what it holds.
- **Layers:** an example reading trail, wind currents for flying, NPC routes, and everything below the cloud sea.
- **Notes:** what's settled, what already works in a test world, and ideas for later.
- **Working notes** (`draft/`): adding full public-domain books from Project Gutenberg, a Literature tower, and storage.

Everything is a work in progress. Isle groupings, heights and floor bands are proposals.
