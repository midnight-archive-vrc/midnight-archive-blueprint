Articles

Their place comes from Wikipedia's own topic lists: Animals › Mammals › Carnivores. Related articles sit side by side, so the whole dog family is one shelf. Most-read sit nearest the landing.

Books

Each Gutenberg book carries a Library of Congress class. Non-fiction about dogs (class SF, animal culture) shelves on this floor's outer ring. Dog novels (class PS/PR, literature) live in the Literature tower, and show up here as cross-links, so The Call of the Wild is one press away from the Wolf article.

Links

Gold links land on this shelf (instant). Blue links fly a book in from elsewhere in the archive. With the ~500,000 most-read articles, nearly every link in a book leads somewhere in the archive (an estimate until the build finishes).
