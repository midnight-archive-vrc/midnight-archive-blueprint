The Midnight Archive · reading idea 3

A helper fetches your books

Follow a gold word and your helper runs to that book's tower, brings it back to you, and tosses the book you finished onto a growing heap in your book cart. The heap is your Back history and your trail for the visit. Each visitor picks how their books follow them: a spirit dog, Slipkits, a cart that rolls by itself, or books that float around you. The sample pages were written for this mock-up, not copied from Wikipedia.

Try this: click a gold word in the open book and watch the fetch. Then try ‹ Back (or the B key), Settle in a nook, Leave, and Fast-forward to see the cart overflow. Switch styles at any time: your books stay.

In VRChat

  • VR: point at a gold word and pull the trigger. The helper hands you the new book and takes the old one. For Back, use a Back gesture or reach into the cart (or the cloud) for the previous book.
  • Desktop: click a gold word. A Back key (B here) or the ‹ Back button on the book.
  • One helper per player, on a PlayerObject like the held-book sync.
  • Others see your helper and your pile: a social signal of how deep you went.
  • Quest and perf: other players' helpers are drawn simply and fade out past about 15 m. The pile is one batched mesh with spine colours from data.
  • Cheap motion: throws are scripted arcs, not physics. The helper floats or flies, so it needs no path-finding.
  • Your style is remembered per player (VRChat Persistence). You pick it at the main hall and can change it any time from your book.
  • Same mechanics under every style: the pile is just your list of books, so each new style is art only.

Builds on

  • The spec's Slipkit fetch: the old "Slipkit fetch animation covers the load", now as your own helper.
  • Books from GitHub Pages: the 4 to 5 s fetch run hides the ~5 s download.
  • Clickable gold words: the testbed's overlay hit areas over the words carry over to the book's pages.
  • A− / A+ with re-paging: works the same on a held book.
  • Back history: the heap is that list, made visible.
  • Grabbable books off a lit bay: the same grab works on books in the cart. Bays still light when you stand next to them, never by facing them.
  • Others see the book you hold: the title on the cover, and now on the spines in your heap.

Open questions

  1. Which helper should new visitors start with: the spirit dog, the Slipkits, the cart, or floating books?
  2. Which styles at launch, and should others see your style? All four, or one or two first?
  3. Should others see your pile? Seen by everyone as a social signal, or private to you?
  4. Does the pile stay where you leave it until you exit the instance, or does the helper always tidy it away when you walk off?
