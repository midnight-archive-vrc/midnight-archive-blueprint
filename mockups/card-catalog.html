The card catalog

Search becomes furniture: an oak card catalog in the main hall with a brass typewriter on top. Type a title on the paper, press Return or step away, and the right drawer slides out with its index cards. Pick a card and the book flies to the reading stand.

Type it for me:

Try it: click the paper on the typewriter and type, then press Enter or click anywhere else. There is no Search button, and typing alone does not search. You can also press the drawn keys and pull the return lever on the left. Click a card to fetch its book, then click a red word in the book to follow it.

The sample index and page text were written for this mock-up, not taken from Wikipedia.

In VRChat

  • VR (hands): press the typewriter keys with a fingertip, or point at the paper to open VRChat's keyboard. Closing the keyboard, pulling the return lever or walking away from the typewriter runs the search. Take a card with your hand to fetch its book.
  • Desktop: click the paper and type. Enter or clicking away searches. Click a card.
  • Quest: the cabinet and typewriter are one baked mesh. Only the open drawer, up to six card quads with one short text each, and one flying book move. Cheap.
  • Where: in the main hall at the centre of the world, and maybe a smaller one in each tower.
  • Beside every lectern: A smaller version stands beside every lectern in the main hall and fetches books straight onto that lectern.
  • Search: it uses the existing search shards and aliases. The drawer that opens is the one for the first letters you typed.

Builds on

  • Search shards and aliases from the testbed, shown on the cards as "AI → Artificial intelligence".
  • Books fetched from GitHub Pages: the ~5 s load hides behind the card flip and the book's flight.
  • Clickable gold words (overlay hit areas over the words) on the book's pages, so the rabbit hole goes on from the stand.
  • A−/A+ text size with re-paging, and Back history, once the book is open.
  • Grabbable books: you can lift the book off the stand like one off a lit bay (bays light when you stand next to them, never by facing them). Others see the book you hold, with its title on the cover.

Open questions

  1. One catalog in the main hall, or one in every tower too? A tower catalog could search only its own tower.
  2. When you pick a card, should the book fly straight to you, or should you be teleported to its shelf? Or both, as two buttons on the card.
  3. Is the typewriter shared? Either everyone nearby sees what is typed and the cards, or each person sees only their own search.
