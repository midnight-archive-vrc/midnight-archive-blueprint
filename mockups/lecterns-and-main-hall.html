Lecterns: reading together, and the main hall

Away from the centre of the world you read real books. The flat reader survives in one place, the main hall on the academy isle: a big central lectern and a ring of smaller sub-lecterns, each shared with everyone in the instance, each with a small search stand beside it. A lecture room off the hall adds a presenter lectern. In the towers, a reading nook's lectern holds one big open book that everyone around it shares.

In VRChat

Main hall (A)

  • Every lectern is one synced object (book id, page, holder). Whatever is open on it, everyone in the instance sees the same thing. Anyone can walk up and use a free one.
  • The central reader works on desktop and Quest exactly like today's panel. A−/A+ stays your own and re-pages for you; the book and the place in it are shared.
  • VR: step up to a lectern (it lights when you stand next to it, never by facing it), point and pull the trigger on words and buttons. Desktop: walk up and click.
  • Mini search: type on the brass typewriter; it searches on Enter or when you leave the field. Cards pop out of the drawer; pick one and the book lands on that lectern, synced like the lectern. It uses the same search shards and aliases as the big catalog.

Reading nook (B)

  • The page and book are synced for everyone in the nook. Anyone can turn the page, and the owner (whoever placed the book) can lock it.
  • VR: lift a book off the cushion and set it on the lectern, then press a turn button or a gold word. Desktop: click them.

Presenter mode (C)

  • Syncs the page and the highlight, and only the current presenter can change them. Hand over passes control to someone in the audience. In presenter mode only the presenter can use the search stand.
  • The audience sees the big page but can't turn it. "Follow along in my own book" shows a small held book that mirrors it.

Everywhere

  • Late joiners see the current book and page at once.
  • Everything is added the same way on PC and Quest, so the network IDs match.
  • Quest cost: low. Each lectern is a page, a text mesh and a few synced values that only send when they change.
  • Lectern look: A3 Candlelit (picked for the lecterns).

Builds on

  • Clickable gold words (overlay hit areas over the words), on every lectern.
  • A−/A+ text size with re-paging, on the central reader.
  • Back history, on the central reader and the nook lectern.
  • Grabbable books off a lit bay: the nook's cushion books are the same grab, and lecterns light the same way (when you stand next to them, never by facing them).
  • Others see the book you hold (title on the cover): the follow-along book and the nook's cover show the title.
  • Books fetched from GitHub Pages with the ~5 s load hidden behind animation: books glide in from the hall's shelves, through the nook's window, or out of the search drawer.
  • People here and music on the central reader, as today.
  • The search tool: each stand is a smaller version of the big card catalog (mock-up 05).
