The Midnight Archive · reading idea 6

The book unfolds

Open a book and parchment leaves fan out around you in three groups: this book (its opening pages), where it leads (one leaf per gold word) and on this shelf (the other books that came in the same file). Everything here follows the world's real data and download limits.

top view

Downloads · one file every 5 s
    In your hands: Octopus

    All book text here was written for this mock-up, not taken from Wikipedia. Two levels are written: Octopus, every book it links to, and the 15 other books on its shelf. Books one step further open a placeholder page that says so. Download timings are simulated with the real rules: one file every 5 s, each arriving 0.2 to 1.5 s after its request, and the last 12 shelves cached.

    Comfort: the arc follows your head

    You are:

    • Standing or seated: the arc opens around your head, its centre about 12° below your eye line, so you read with a relaxed neck.
    • Lying back: the arc opens overhead, facing down at you.
    • Dragging your playspace: the leaves stay frozen in place while you move, then gently re-centre once you have been still for about 0.4 s.
    • Walking or flying: the arc never moves while you walk or fly.
    • Near a wall: the arc shrinks its radius so no leaf sits inside the wall.
    • All of this uses VRChat's head tracking; nothing else is needed.

    In VRChat

    • VR: open the book in your hands (best at a lectern or in a nook) and its leaves spread around you at reading distance. Grab a page leaf to pull it close. Grab a link or shelf leaf to open that book. Three ribbons on the book switch the groups.
    • Desktop: look around and click a leaf; arrow keys turn the arc; click a ribbon to switch groups.
    • Quest: at most about 7 leaf quads exist at once, with all their text on one shared canvas texture. Turning the arc reuses the quads for the next leaves. Pictures appear only on the open book.
    • Downloads: one file every 5 s per visitor. A shelf file (16 books, about 40 KB) arrives 0.2 to 1.5 s after it is asked for, and the last ~12 shelves stay cached. Link leaves show title and shelf at once and fill in shelf by shelf. Links on one shelf fill together, and the leaf you look at goes first.
    • Pictures: about 60% of books have one. They only show for visitors with "Allow untrusted URLs" on, and each picture is a download too, so here it waits behind the text.
    • Not while flying: the arc is for reading in one place.

    Builds on

    • Shelf files on GitHub Pages: 16 books per JSON, each with its lead (up to 2,000 characters, already split into 1 to 4 pages). "This book" is those pages; "On this shelf" is the other 15 books from the same file.
    • Clickable gold words (overlay hit areas): each link already carries its target's id and title, so link leaves show titles at once.
    • The id to tower/level/shelf table baked into the world: every link leaf shows where its book lives before anything downloads.
    • A− / A+ with re-paging applies to the page leaves.
    • Back history: Back returns to the previous book, usually from cache, so it is instant.
    • Grabbable books off a lit bay (bays light when you stand next to them, never by facing them) and others see the book you hold (title on the cover): unchanged.
    • ~5 s loads hidden behind animation: a book whose shelf is not here yet flies in from its tower, and the flight lasts exactly as long as the download.
