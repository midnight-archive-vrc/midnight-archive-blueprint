The book is the reader

You read from the actual book in your hands, not a floating panel. Click a glowing spine and it slides out, rises into your hands and opens into a Brass & Vellum spread you can turn, hold closer and resize. Click another bay along the walkway to make it your active shelf.

Where you read: everywhere except the main hall at the centre of the world, you read from books like this one. The old flat panel lives only on the main hall lectern.

In VRChat

  • VR: hold the book with one hand. Flick the page corner with the other hand or the thumbstick. Tap gold words with a fingertip or the laser. Holding it closer is just moving your hand.
  • Picking: Pick a book by grabbing or clicking it. A shelf becomes active when you stand next to it or click it.
  • Desktop: the held book sits in front of the camera, and you click words and corners.
  • Quest: one open book per player, so only one book's pages and text are live per person.

Builds on

  • Clickable gold words (overlay hit areas over the words) and the "→ Predation" bubble.
  • A− / A+ text size with re-paging: the spread count changes as the text re-flows.
  • Back history after following a gold word.
  • Grabbable books off a lit bay. Bays light when you stand next to them, never by facing them.
  • Others see the book you hold, title on the cover.
  • Books fetched from GitHub Pages; the ~5 s load hides behind the slide-out, rise and opening (the fetch can start when you step up to the bay).

Open questions

  1. Flying: does the book stay open in your hands while you fly, or close and hang at your side until you land?
  2. Pages: two pages, or one big page at a time? Two pages feel like a real book; one big page fits bigger text and longer lines.
