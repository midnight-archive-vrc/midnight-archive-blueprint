The Midnight Archive · reading idea 2

Touch a gold word, the book comes to you

You read from a real book held in your hand. Touch one of its gold words and a thread of light runs out to the tower where that book lives; the book flies back along it, lands in your free hand and opens. The flight takes about four seconds, which hides the ~5 s page download.

Hover a gold word to see where it leads (try predators). Click any gold word to fetch its book. Try blood, ocean or ink, then keep following links from the new book.
Reading Octopus.

The sample pages were written for this mock-up, not copied from Wikipedia. Every gold word leads to another sample book, so you can keep hopping.

In VRChat

VR

Touch a gold word with a fingertip (invisible hit areas sit over each word), or point the laser at it and pull the trigger. The book lands in your free hand.

Desktop

Click the word. The book flies to the empty side of your view and opens there; the old one drops to your side.

Quest

The thread is a single line renderer and the flying book is one small mesh with its title on the cover. The full page is only built once the book lands.

Other players

The flying book is visible to everyone, so it advertises where you are going. A book crossing the sky shows off the rabbit hole.

Builds on

  • Clickable gold words: the same overlay hit areas over each word; a fingertip touch and a laser click both land on them.
  • Books fetched from GitHub Pages, ~5 s load hidden behind animation: here the flight is that animation, and the cover shimmers until the page is ready.
  • Grabbable books off a lit bay: the arriving book is the same grabbable book, delivered to your hand instead of taken off a shelf. Bays still wake when you stand next to them, never by facing them.
  • Others see the book you hold, title on the cover: the flying book carries its title too.
  • Back history: every hop goes on the Back list; the book at your side is the last step back.
  • A− / A+ text size with re-paging: works the same in whichever book is open.

Open questions

  1. Does the new book replace the one in your hand, or arrive in your free hand? The demo uses the free hand and swaps hands on each hop.
  2. Should other players see the thread, or only the flying book?
  3. What happens to the old book? Here it shrinks to your side and the one before it fades away. Idea 3, a pile of books as Back, answers this another way.
