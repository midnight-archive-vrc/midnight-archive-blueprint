Midnight Archive · the Brass & Vellum reader, six ways

The Brass & Vellum reader as first drawn (A1) and five variations of it. In every one the gold words in the text are the links: click a word to open that book. A− / A+ changes the text size: the page re-flows, shows whole lines only, and the page count changes (in the world each player's size is remembered for their next visit). Hover "predators" (or point at it in VR) to see where a link goes when its words don't name the book. The sample text is written for this mock-up, not taken from Wikipedia.

Parchment page in a walnut frame with brass corners, ink-red links, a drop cap, brass buttons.
